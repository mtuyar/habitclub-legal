Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and enter your email address. The link you receive opens the app and lets you set a new password. If no email arrives, check your spam folder.

I lost my streak

A streak resets when no task is completed on a given day. It is calculated at midnight in your device's time zone. If you believe it was calculated incorrectly, write to us.

I'm not receiving notifications

Make sure notifications are allowed for Habit Club in your phone's settings, then enable them in the app under Profile → Notifications. Low power mode and battery optimisation can delay delivery.

How do I leave a room?

Open the room, tap the settings icon in the top right and choose Leave room. If you created the room, it will be deleted.

I saw offensive content or a user

Long-press a chat message, or use Report from a member's profile or the room settings. From the same places you can block a user — a blocked person cannot message you, nudge you, or invite you to a room. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

What does the AI coach do?

It reads your last seven days and writes a comment specific to you. It sends nothing until you opt in. Exactly what is sent is listed on the consent screen and in the privacy policy.

I want to delete my account

Follow the steps on the account deletion page.

When is premium coming?

Premium features are not on sale yet and appear as "coming soon" in the app. This version contains no in-app purchases.
